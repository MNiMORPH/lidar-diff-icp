# FRAME 2026-08-26 — linking gen1 to gen2 for a better DoD  (READ FIRST after compaction)

## Goal
Bring gen1 (2008 leaf-off) elevations into gen2's (2021 leaf-on) frame under forest, so the
DoD stops reading false aggradation on forested hillslopes. Long game: a deterministic,
reusable correction for MN-wide gen1. Elba/elbaext are the pilot tiles.

## VERIFIED TODAY — do not re-derive
1. **Registration is done and tested.** Four terms now carried per return in
   `beam_offset_table.parquet` (both tiles): `dz_geoid_mm`, `dz_lateral_mm`, `dz_swath_mm`,
   `dz_drift_mm`, summed into **`d_mm_corr`**. In-grid median offset: elba −55.6 → **−5.3 mm**,
   elbaext −80.6 → **−0.9 mm**. Open non-eroding ground closes to 2–5 mm.
   `src/lidar_diff_icp/registration.py` + `tests/test_registration.py` (21 tests, mutation-proven).
2. **Incidence/slope is NOT a driver.** The old "+3.5 mm/deg incidence" was per-swath
   misalignment: within-cell slope +4.29 → −0.03 (elba), +3.63 → −0.07 (elbaext) once the
   swath term is applied. The ~27° knee was the same artifact. **Slope is not a variable**;
   everything is slope-normal already. Verified: holding cover fixed, offset is flat 0–36°.
3. **Cover IS the organising variable.** On no-change ground (divides, |curv|≤0.002,
   incidence<5°): ~0 open, −20 mm at cover 0.3, −60 to −130 mm above 0.5. Two tiles agree.
   Isotonic (shape-free) curve is the fit; no functional form is distinguishable once errors
   are cluster-robust.
4. **Forest type matters.** Conifer/brush (canopy in BOTH epochs) sits −19.7 ± 7.2 mm below
   deciduous at matched cover. Above cover 0.5, 80–94% of cells are conifer-like, so "cover"
   and "type" are confounded at Elba. `gen{1,2}_canopy_frac.npz` per tile hold the per-epoch
   canopy return fraction (identical definition both epochs).
5. **Phenology (leaf-off penetrates deeper) is REFUTED as the driver** — deciduous is the
   LESS affected class. Offset tracks the 2008 canopy, not 2021 cover.

## RESULTS ON THE VERIFIED CUBE
- **Best metric so far: matched median.** `p50(gen1) − p50(gen2)` + registration:
  RMS **98.7 mm both tiles** vs current pipeline (CSF cloth vs class-2) 111.4 / 109.4.
  ~11% better, needs NO classifier. Optimum broad and identical on both tiles (p44–p52).
- **Best single gen1 percentile to estimate gen2 ground**: q ≈ 0.47–0.61 (median criterion).
  med|r| 36 mm open, 65–73 mm vegetated; RMS 81 mm open, 125–146 mm vegetated.
- **Q-Q, Q2(q) = a + b·Q1(q)**: open is location-scale (R² 0.995–0.997, b ≈ 0.34, |dev| ≤ 5 mm).
  Vegetated R² ≈ 0.57–0.62, b ≈ 1.5–1.6, residuals monotone +180 → −165 mm over q=0.05→0.85,
  then +600–690 mm at q=0.95. Both tiles agree to ~20 mm at every quantile.
- **Per-cell noise floor**: ~48 mm median, ~108–122 mm RMS on no-change ground. The cover
  effect (−5 to −60 mm) sits BELOW this per cell — it only appears in medians over 1000s of cells.

## NEGATIVES (do not re-walk)
- Whole-distribution L-estimator, per-cell, on the corrected cube: **ties** the CSF cloth
  (RMS 107.5/106.9 vs 109.1). The earlier 40–50% win was on the BUGGY cube (spatial pooling
  was averaging away terrain contamination) — not evidence.
- Spatial pooling over raw neighbourhoods mixes unlike cells; if pooling is needed, pool over
  cells MATCHED in cover, never raw space.
- L2 fitting gives a ~10 mm MEDIAN bias (right-skewed residuals). Use L1.
- Return-count thresholds do not filter quality — they silently re-select toward open canopy.
- Smoothing the cover raster (15–75 m) does not help; native 5 m is the better covariate.
