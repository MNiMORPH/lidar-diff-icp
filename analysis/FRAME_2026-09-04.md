## The method (settled, documented, reproducible)

`analysis/GROUND_Q_FROM_CLASS2_SPREAD.md` is the authority. In short: the pipeline took the
per-cell MEDIAN of class-2 returns as the ground (`ground_q = 0.50`). Measured at 519 gen2
control marks, that is right where the ground class is clean and wrong where it is not, and
**the spread of the class-2 returns says which is which** — no cover product, no windows, no
external layer.

    rank of surveyed ground within class-2 returns: median 0.4532, class-2 median +8.1 mm high
    FLAT below ~60 mm of class-2 spread (rho +0.006, p 0.94), FALLING above (rho -0.183, p 0.001)
    the break sits at the 59.3 mm bare-ground class-2 NMAD -- the surface's own noise
    curve: q = 0.506 at 20-40 mm, 0.445 at 60, 0.395 at 120, 0.237 at 200, 0.101 at 400 mm
    held out, 10 km spatially blocked: +8.1 mm bias -> -2.4, RMS 124.5 -> 104.6 mm

Reproduce every number in the doc:

    ./lidar-icp/bin/python analysis/calibrate_ground_q.py --set gen2_2021_control --diagnostics
