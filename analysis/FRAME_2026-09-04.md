## THE METHOD, as settled 2026-09-04 (Andy: "we have found our new method")

Two separable things. The base pipeline does not depend on the correction.

**BASE — the DoD, the LoD, the registration.** Needs a no-change population, and that
population is now:

    floodplain  cut by ELEVATION, never TPI. valley_top_m if stated, else the
                VALLEY_TOP_M registry, else the first local minimum ABOVE the tile's
                dominant elevation mode. Every run prints which and how many cells.
    stable      |laplacian| <= curv_max (0.005, MINE) AND above the valley top.
                NO slope restriction and NOT ridgetops -- the lateral Nuth-Kaeae shift

**VEGETATION CORRECTION — optional, and it needs NO no-change population.** It is
calibrated against surveyed control marks, so nothing in it assumes ground has not moved.
groundq / class2_spread_grid / dod_cover_corrected contain zero references to
reference_cells. Chain: control marks -> (spread, rank) -> piecewise q(SD) -> per-cell
percentile off the class-2 column. Off by default: difference_dem(ground_q=0.50), and
"calibrated" requires gen2_curve by name.

WHAT THE CORRECTION IS AND IS NOT. Held out at the marks it is real: RMS 124.5 -> 101.5 mm,
p90 171.0 -> 152.9. On the tile it barely moves the forest-open contrast on divide cells
(+2.06 mm of 58 at elba, -0.51 of 39 at whitewater), because forest and open there both sit
on the curve's flat segment -- the differential available is ~1.8 mm. Where we can assume no
change the ground is clean and there is little to do; where it acts we cannot assume no
change. That is the honest state, not a failure to be tuned away.

## The method — one object, two halves, in `src/lidar_diff_icp/groundq.py`

`analysis/GROUND_Q_FROM_CLASS2_SPREAD.md` is the authority on the derivation. The code:

| half | functions |
|---|---|
| calibrate | `mark_statistics` · `spatial_folds` · `fit_curve` · `save_curve` |
| apply | `surface_from_grid` · `reference_surface` · `column_histogram` · `spread_from_histogram` · `ground_at_q` · `ground_at_median` · `correct_gen2` |

They must measure the SAME statistic — the plain SD of the ground-class column about the
local surface — or the curve is read at the wrong place on its own x-axis.
`tests/test_groundq.py` pins that, and pins that the robust spread which was briefly used
instead disagrees by more than the calibrated range is wide.

    rank of surveyed ground within class-2 returns: median 0.4532, class-2 median +8.1 mm high
    curve: q = 0.506 at 20-40 mm, 0.445 at 60, 0.395 at 120, 0.237 at 200, 0.101 at 400 mm
    held out, 10 km spatially blocked: +8.1 mm bias -> -2.4, RMS 124.5 -> 104.6 mm

Reproduce every number, and the curve byte-identically:

    ./lidar-icp/bin/python analysis/calibrate_ground_q.py --set gen2_2021_control --diagnostics

## Callers — there is no second implementation

- `analysis/ridgelines/dod_cover_corrected.py --q-from-class2-spread` — the TILE route.
  Verified byte-identical across the lift on all four Elba arrays.
- `pipeline.difference_dem(ground_q="calibrated")` — applies it as a post-registration
  DELTA, `h2(calibrated) - h2(median)`, so (1) the gen1 tie is fitted against the
  UNCORRECTED surface and cannot absorb the correction, and (2) the frame cancels exactly
  rather than smuggling in the low_q-vs-slope_normal difference. Second pass by necessity:
  the reference surface IS the q = 0.50 grid.
- `_stream_ground` is back to a plain quantile. The retrofit is deleted.

## THE RESULT — the correction is measured, and NOT adopted

`analysis/GROUND_Q_FROM_CLASS2_SPREAD.md` is the authority and was rewritten 2026-09-04 to
lead with this. Held out on 10 km blocks over the 227 NVA (open-ground) marks:

    q = 0.50 (pipeline default)             median err -3.5 mm   RMS 49.1 mm
    q = 0.527 constant (calibrated)         median err  0.1      RMS 48.7
    q = isotonic(log class-2 SD), held out  median err -5.8      RMS 52.5

The curve is WORSE than doing nothing on open ground. The 124.5 -> 104.6 mm gain that once
justified it was measured on all 519 marks POOLED, and the control set is three populations:

    NVA n=227  class-2 median  -3.5 mm from truth   open ground
    VVA n=162                +103.3 mm              sited UNDER VEGETATION, by design
    LCP n=130                 -23.1 mm              the acquisition's calibration points

The pooled curve's whole falling limb is the VVA marks. Applied, it widened the DoD's scatter
at both sites: Elba NMAD 74.77 -> 79.09 mm, Whitewater 85.01 -> 92.35 on common cells.

Found by re-measuring on Whitewater's own flight lines (psid 3029-3033): 9 marks touch them,
ranks bimodal with 0 of 9 in the middle band against 65.3% pooled, binomial
p = 7.260162231249149e-05 -- and the split is WITHIN lines, so it is point_type, not geometry.

**This broke a rule the project already had.** `ground_control/run_bridge_gen2.py` makes
`--point-types` required ("--point-types NVA is the consequence, not a preference");
`README.md` lists "open ground only" among three governing rules;
`analysis/control_lowveg_offset.py` excludes LCPs. The calibration pooled all three and
recorded the choice nowhere.

## The contract now, after the docs were re-led from the code

- `difference_dem(ground_q=0.50)` is the DEFAULT again, and is the best open-ground estimate.
- `ground_q="calibrated"` REQUIRES `gen2_curve=<path>`. No default curve exists.
- `calibrate_ground_q.py --point-types` is required, with no default; the types go in the
  filename and the provenance; `load_curve` REFUSES a curve that records none.
- `workflow.BASE_GLOBAL_INPUTS` is empty: a tile's ground depends on nothing outside the tile.
- The untyped pooled curve is retired from the tree; `..._LCP-NVA-VVA.npz` reproduces it
  bit-for-bit and says what it is.
