## THE MAIN RESULT OF THIS SESSION

**gen2's delivered ground surface floats above true ground in proportion to near-ground
vegetation, measured on 389 surveyed checkpoints, single epoch, no DoD, no erosion possible.**

```
offset_mm = block_intercept + (-268.2 +- 31.6) * lowveg          CV R2 0.195

  MN_SEDriftless_1_2021   -35.60 +- 7.93        (n=77)
  MN_SEDriftless_2_2021   -14.49 +- 7.21  <- Elba   (n=89)
  MN_SEDriftless_3_2021   -10.26 +- 8.00        (n=71)
  MN_SEDriftless_4_2021   +10.67 +- 7.35        (n=77)
  MN_SEDriftless_5_2021    -1.52 +- 7.72        (n=75)
```

Sign: `offset = surveyed_Z - delivered_Z`; **negative = the lidar reads HIGH**. Apply by
SUBTRACTING `268 * lowveg` mm from the delivered gen2 surface. For Elba, `-14.5 - 268.2*lowveg`.

The **slope is physics** (shared across all five blocks, every one negative, four significant
despite spanning 2021-04-16 to 2022-06-05). The **intercepts are datum** — five separate
acquisitions, each with its own unpublished vendor bias adjustment.

### `lowveg`, the definition that must travel with the coefficient

Fraction of returns with slope-normal height in **(0.15, 2.0] m** above an order-2
least-squares surface fitted to **class-2** returns within **7.5 m**, with the denominator
being returns in **(-1.0, +2.0] m** — the NEAR-GROUND population, not all returns. It is a
COMPOSITION, not a canopy density. Because the surface comes from the box's own returns,
`lowveg` is invariant to any vertical shift of the cloud and therefore structurally cannot
carry the offset it predicts.

**It is ORDINAL.** Moving the lower edge +-0.10 m changes its value ~50x while the rank
correlation moves 0.07. A slope in mm-per-unit is meaningless detached from this definition.

**Known improvement not yet adopted:** a 20-seed sweep put the optimal upper edge at 4-8 m
(CV R2 ~0.199) rather than 2.0 (0.184). 2.0 was inherited from the histogram's edge, not
chosen. Adopting it requires splicing the canopy window; the definition, the report and the
fitted coefficient would all need updating together.

## NEGATIVES — do not re-walk these

Every one is measured, most on held-out spatial blocks with 20 fold seeds (sd ~0.007).

- **Canopy cover fails**: `rho -0.053, p 0.29`. `min_height=2.0` and `voxel_z=1.0` make
  sub-metre vegetation invisible; `mh=0.15` and `mh=0.5` are identical row-for-row because
  the voxel height, not the threshold, is binding.
- **Nothing beats `lowveg`**: not scatter, peak width, skewness, entropy, Wasserstein
  distance to the bare-ground profile, PCA scores, or ridge on up to 488 bins (best 0.166
  against one scalar's 0.170). Two independent searches converged.
- **The shape carries nothing beyond the amount.** Tail decay length, shape ratio, core
  width, Bowley/Pearson/moment skewness — all null under partial correlation AND on held-out
  prediction. FPCA says why: PC1 is 62.9% of variance and IS peak-to-fringe mass transfer.
- **Canopy adds nothing**: 188 canopy bins give CV R2 0.016; splicing them onto the
  near-ground profile DEGRADES it (0.154 -> 0.084).
- **THE UNIFYING FAILURE — four constructions, one cause.** `h=0` is the LS surface through
  class-2, which vegetation has pulled UP. So true ground sits below it, and any method that
  estimates the ground population from the data and subtracts it removes the signal:
  mirror-subtraction (mass `rho -0.026`), fixed-sigma mixture (KS 0.197, misfit ∝ vegetation
  +0.718), lower-half fit (`sigma_low` ∝ vegetation +0.770, excess null), and the free-sigma
  convolution below (`s` ∝ vegetation +0.905, `f` collapsed to a bound in 27% of marks — it
  fixes the DENSITY and not the identifiability). **Within a single mark, ground width and
  vegetation are the same measurement.** Only external sigma breaks it.
  This is why the band fraction wins: it counts mass above a FIXED height and never models
  the ground population, so a displaced reference cannot cancel it.
- **Pooling manufactures heavy tails.** The pooled excess looks power-law; per mark the tail
  is exponential in 83% of marks by median dAIC 44. Per-mark `lambda` spans 42-353 mm, and a
  superposition of exponentials with a broad rate distribution gives a power-law-looking pool.
  The project's earlier "forest exponential / open power-law" finding was per-STRATUM and
  deserves the same check.
- **`lambda` correlates but does not predict**: `rho -0.222, p 1e-05` (fixed-sigma fit),
  CV R2 -0.007. I first reported this as "no correlation" by quoting Spearman on the
  convolution fit where 33% of values are tied at a bound — Pearson there is `-0.164,
  p 1.2e-03`. **Never quote a rank statistic on bound-piled data.**

## MODEL FITTING — where it stands

- Best-fitting density is the **convolution** (agent's idea, unpublished): every return carries
  the ranging error, so `p(h) = (1-f)N(0,s) + f[N(0,s) (x) Exp(lam)]` — an EMG tail. KS 0.075
  vs 0.083 (single Gaussian) vs 0.197 (fixed-sigma mixture), and misfit-vs-vegetation falls
  +0.718 -> +0.166. **It fixes the density and NOT the identifiability**: with `s` free,
  `s` vs vegetation is +0.905 and `f` collapses (median 0.05, at a bound 27%, `rho -0.015`).
- Single Gaussian: SD median 88.9 mm (bare-ground sigma 34.2). Misfit is one-sidedness — the
  tail inflates SD, the symmetric fit then invents mass BELOW ground (-0.147) and flattens
  the peak (+1.07). SD vs offset `rho -0.317`, CV R2 0.121.
- **`analysis/GROUND_RETURN_PDF_LITERATURE.md`** (commit `c678490`): there is **no published
  PDF** for the vertical distribution of discrete returns about a ground surface. Gaussian is
  conventional, not derived (Chauve 2007 §3.3 says so in print). The exponential tail IS
  derived from Beer-Lambert with a vertical-UNIFORMITY assumption that a near-ground layer
  violates. DTM-error non-normality is established but the field abandoned parametric
  description, so **KS has no published benchmark** and rejects at our n regardless — the
  vegetation-correlation of the misfit is the real diagnostic, not the KS level. Identifiability
  failure is textbook (Day 1969; McLachlan & Peel 2000); Hathaway 1985 = constrain the scale
  RATIO. Primary recommendation: make sigma **measured per site from Glennie/Gardner geometry**,
  not fitted.
