## What changed on 2026-09-05: difference_dem was split into named stages

`difference_dem` went 695 → 316 lines. It is now an assembly of seven functions, each
independently callable and tested. **Every one was gated by an Elba rebuild that reproduced
`dod.npy`, `lod.npy`, `z_after.npy`, `change.npy` and `corrections.json` BYTE-IDENTICALLY** —
eight consecutive gates. The baseline is
`<scratchpad>/elba_prerefactor/` (Sep 4 22:57).

    terrain.terrain_masks        slope, curvature, valley cut, stable mask
    groundest.estimate_ground    the four ground estimators (cellstat, plane, poly2,
                                 slope_normal)
    groundq.resolve_ground_q     ground_q -> (scalar, curve); every refusal
    pipeline.grid_reference      the gen2 reference grid: ground/spread/count/rough
    pipeline.register_gen1       CSF -> boresight -> swath network. GEN1-INTERNAL
    pipeline.apply_datum         lateral Nuth-Kaeaeb shift, then the geoid. CROSS-EPOCH
    pipeline.correct_reference   the OPTIONAL vegetation correction, as a delta
    pipeline.difference          the DoD and its reporting stable population
    pipeline.estimate_lod        the per-cell limit of detection

Two of these separations are load-bearing and are pinned by tests, not just comments:

* **register_gen1 reads no gen2 data.** If it did, the gen1 swath tie could absorb a
  cross-epoch correction. A test asserts the signature carries no gen2 argument.
* **`spread` and `rough` are different numbers.** `spread` is taken about the cell's own
  horizontal level so on a slope it counts the slope; `rough` is the residual to a fitted
  plane and does not. The ground-q curve is indexed by `spread`; the LoD's standard error is
  built from `rough`. Swapping them is silent and biases every corrected elevation on a
  slope. A test fails if they are swapped.

`apply_datum` MUTATES x, y, z in place and returns the same arrays — kept deliberately (tens
of millions of points at statewide scale), documented, and pinned by a test.
