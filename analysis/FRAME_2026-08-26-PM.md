## What was established today (results WITH their method)

**Absolute datum, new.** gen1 is **+22.7 mm low**, σ_total 39.7 mm, from two surveyed 3DEP
marks reached by swath chains: 2210 +21.3 ± 12.4 (west, 5 links, 128→133) and 2036 +28.9 ± 27.0
(east, 6 links, 144→138). They agree to 7.5 mm, but that is a consistency check, not the
accuracy — and they arrive on DIFFERENT swaths, comparable only through elbaext's 133↔138 link,
the worst-constrained one. Product `data/derived/elba_fulldensity/z_before_absolute.npy` with a
sidecar. Method + sign conventions in `docs/groundtruth.md`; `tie_mm = surveyed − z_lidar`, so
positive = lidar low. Module `src/lidar_diff_icp/groundtruth/`, 154 tests pass.

**gen2 against its own six marks** (never run before, no chain needed — same datum): NVA median
+17.2 mm low. So gen2 is not systematically low, the gen1 constant is gen1's own, and gen2 really
is on GEOID18. **But the four NVA marks span 103 mm, 40.8 mm RMS** — two fail 3DEP's own 35 mm
RMSEz on radius spread alone. **The mark and its terrain set the floor, not the lidar**; the
monuments sit on local highs (at 2210 the surveyed height is the p95 of gen1 returns within 5 m).

**Do NOT shift the DoD by the tie difference.** Correcting both epochs moves the DoD by only
22.7 − 17.2 = 5.5 mm, but paired at the same marks the epoch residual is +22.0 (2210) and −30.5
(2036) — opposite signs. The divides give gen2 − gen1 = −4.62 ± 1.93 mm on 24,487 cells, 20×
more precise and opposite in sign to the marks. Marks pin the LEVEL; divides pin the EPOCH
DIFFERENCE. Applying −5.5 would move the stable-divide DoD from −4.62 to −10.1.

**Canopy correction, shipped.** `q2 = 0.5 − 0.19·cover` — take gen2's ground at that percentile
of its own class-2 near-ground column instead of the median. `analysis/ridgelines/Q2_COVER_RELATION.md`;
product `dod_cover_q2.npy` + `.json`, control `dod_gen2_median.npy`, `gen2_q2_used.npy`. Removes
the false forest aggradation: forest−open +23.4 → −1.1 mm, residual ≤5 mm at every cover level

## NEGATIVES — do not re-walk

- **Classifier choice does not matter.** gen1 CSF vs vendor TerraScan: 0.0 mm in every cover
  stratum. gen2 re-classified with our CSF: +1.1 to +5.6 mm, wrong sign to help.
- **CSF parameters do not matter for the surface.** rigidness 1 vs 3: −0.0 to −0.2 mm. threshold
  1.5 → 0.5: −3 mm, WORSE. `filters.outlier` on: 0.0 mm. rigidness 2 / cloth 2.5: slightly worse.
- **The percentile route is closed for the general problem.** Both epochs' ground columns are
  symmetric at every cover level (|Bowley skew| ≤ 0.048), so the offset is a pure translation and
  no order statistic recovers it. Width saturates while need grows 20×.
- **Mission-time drift: rejected.** Line ordinal beats gps_time at 6 of 7 sites; gaps go the wrong
  way (elba's 6,290 s gap gives +11.2 mm, its 1,022 s gap +23.9); Battle Creek's 19.7-hour break
  gives its smallest step; rate spans +46.9 to −74.7 mm/h and reverses sign. **Keep the per-swath
  constants as they are** — neither a time nor an ordinal model beats them against a 12.4 mm
  repeatability bar. `analysis/MISSION_TIME_DRIFT.md`.
- **Aircraft orientation: no evidence.** Headings alternate strictly S/N but dz ramps monotonically;
  heading term +1.3 mm at 0.46σ. Weak test though — n = 5 swaths, one site, because only elba and
  elbaext have a `beam_offset_table.parquet`. 42 more swaths across 10 sites could test it.
- **Re-referencing the swath solution cannot help.** `align_swaths` solves the free network and
  subtracts the reference afterwards (`coreg.py:485`), so re-referencing shifts every constant by
  one number and changes no difference (verified to 3.5e−15 mm).
- **Incidence is not a driver, under canopy either.** Within-cell slope of offset on incidence goes
  +4.4 → +0.3 mm/deg at every cover level once corrected. A POOLED cross-cell table cannot separate
  a beam effect from a cell-selection effect — always run the within-cell version.
