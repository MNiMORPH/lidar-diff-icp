## WHY THIS WORK EXISTS (origin and trajectory)

DoD = gen2 (USGS 3DEP 2021) - gen1 (MN DNR 2008), 5 m, EPSG:26915, pilot tile elbaext.
The floodplain read as ~ -120 mm of erosion. This session established that it is a
VEGETATION ARTIFACT, not erosion and not the correction surface, and then rebuilt the
registration so the product carries no fitted spatial field. The open problem is that BOTH
epochs' near-ground vegetation biases the ground estimate, in OPPOSITE directions, and the
estimator we grid with (a median) cannot separate ground from vegetation. Mixture
separation is the proposed fix.
